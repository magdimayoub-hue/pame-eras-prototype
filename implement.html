How to start

Five steps to a working programme.

Start with one procedure, prove the benefit, then scale.

1

Build the team

A clinical lead from surgery and anaesthesia, a nurse coordinator, and support from management.

  • Name an ERAS coordinator
  • Secure time and a small budget
2

Choose a procedure and a protocol

Pick one high-volume operation and adapt the matching ERAS® Society guideline to your hospital.

  • Colorectal, caesarean or joint replacement are common starts
  • Agree each element with every discipline
3

Measure your baseline

Audit current practice and outcomes before changing anything, so you can prove the difference.

  • Length of stay, complications, readmissions
  • Compliance with each protocol element
4

Train and launch

Teach the whole pathway: clinic, ward, theatre and recovery staff, and the patients themselves.

  • Patient information in local languages
  • Order sets and checklists
5

Audit, feed back, sustain

Review compliance and outcomes regularly, share the results, and expand to new procedures.

  • Monthly team meetings
  • Celebrate results, fix gaps
