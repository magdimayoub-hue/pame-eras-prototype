Enhanced Recovery After Surgery

One team, one pathway, a faster recovery.

ERAS® is an evidence-based, multidisciplinary approach to care around surgery. Small, proven steps before, during and after the operation reduce the body's stress response, so patients get back on their feet sooner.

Shorter staysPatients go home sooner
Fewer complicationsMedical and surgical
Fewer readmissionsRecovery that lasts
Happier patientsPartners in their own care

Learn how ERAS® works

  • Education and shared expectations
  • Optimise nutrition, anaemia and fitness
  • Carbohydrate drink, shorter fasting
  • Minimally invasive surgery where possible
  • Tailored anaesthesia and fluids
  • Opioid-sparing pain relief, warmth
  • Early eating and drinking
  • Out of bed on the same or next day
  • Early removal of drips, drains and catheters
  • Track compliance with each element
  • Compare outcomes over time
  • Feed results back to the whole team

General summary of ERAS® principles. Protocols vary by specialty; follow the published guidelines.
